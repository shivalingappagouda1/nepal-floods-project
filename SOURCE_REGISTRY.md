## 2. Factual Integrity & Investigation Status Rules

1. **Confirmed Facts:**
   - The transboundary geography (Poiqu River originating in Tibet Autonomous Region, China, flowing across the border at Kodari/Tatopani to become the Bhote Koshi / Sun Koshi River in Sindhupalchok, Nepal).
   - Geographic coordinates of the Miteri Friendship Bridge (`27.9575° N, 85.9461° E`) and Barhabise municipal hub (`27.7890° N, 85.8980° E`).
   - The strategic role of Araniko Highway (NH03) as Nepal's primary northern overland trade lifeline to China.
2. **Preliminary / Under Investigation Facts:**
   - Specific triggering mechanism of the 26 August 2026 surge (whether initiated by upstream glacial lake moraine collapse, moraine overtopping, or tributary landslide dam breach) must remain explicitly tagged as:
     `"Preliminary"` or `"Mechanism under investigation"`.
   - Structural damage counts, exact inundation polygons, and casualty estimates must NOT be stated as factual without official verified situation reports from NDRRMA / DHM / UNOSAT.
